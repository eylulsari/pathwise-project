Istanbul's sunset is not something you watch from one place. The city spans two
continents, so the light falls on each neighbourhood from a different angle: in
one it lights the silhouette of the mosques, in another the water of the
Bosphorus, in a third the Golden Horn. The eight below are spread across
neighbourhoods, so one of them is near you whichever side you are on.

## 1. Süleymaniye Mosque — Eminönü

On one of the highest hills in the city. From its courtyard the sunset arrives
together with the silhouette of the mosques — not a single view, but domes and
minarets layered over each other.

## 2. Galata Tower — Karaköy

A 360-degree view: the historic peninsula on one side, the Bosphorus on the
other. This is where looking out from inside the old fabric of the city makes
its difference felt.

## 3. Ortaköy Square — Ortaköy

One of the few places where you can get the mosque and the Bosphorus Bridge
into the same frame. The square gets crowded, but the frame is worth it.

## 4. Çamlıca Hill — Üsküdar

The widest panorama in the city. The height here shows you not individual
buildings but Istanbul as a whole.

## 5. Eyüp Cable Car — Eyüpsultan

A spot reached by cable car, looking over the Golden Horn. The view opening up
as you rise makes the journey part of it.

## 6. Moda Seaside — Kadıköy

A calm, local alternative. A shoreline without the tourist crowd, where you can
sit and wait.

## 7. Aya Yorgi Church, Büyükada — Princes' Islands

A day of its own, and worth the ferry ride. From the top of the island the
sunset begins out over the sea.

## 8. Rumeli Lighthouse — Sarıyer

The northernmost point of the city, opening onto the Black Sea. Where the
Bosphorus ends and the sea begins.
