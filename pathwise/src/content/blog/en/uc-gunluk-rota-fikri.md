The mistake first-time visitors make most often is spreading three days across
the whole city: Sultanahmet in the morning, Kadıköy in the afternoon, Beyoğlu
in the evening. In Istanbul that means spending most of the day in transit. The
skeleton below ties each day to a single area, so it can be walked.

## Day 1 — Sultanahmet

The historic peninsula, and nearly all of it within walking distance.

- **Hagia Sophia**
- **Topkapı Palace**
- **Basilica Cistern**

All three are a few hundred metres apart. The day fills up comfortably here;
do not try to compress it.

## Day 2 — Beyoğlu and Karaköy

The spine of the modern side.

- **İstiklal Avenue**
- **Galata Tower**

You can walk down from İstiklal to Galata, and since that direction is
downhill, take it that way round.

## Day 3 — The Bosphorus line

- **Ortaköy**
- **Bebek**

Walkable along the shore. If you have the time and the weather, you can swap
this day for the ferry to the **Princes' Islands** — but do not put both in the
same day; the island is a day on its own.

## Make this skeleton your own

The above is a starting point, not a rule. Your budget, how far you can walk
and who you came with all change the route — you may have two days instead of
three, or want a day built around food rather than museums.
